MULTI VIEW UNIVERSE — RASPBERRY PI 3 BUTTON WIRING

Use BCM pin numbering (not physical pin numbering). Each momentary push button connects between its listed GPIO pin and any GND pin.
The program uses the Raspberry Pi internal pull-up resistor. Do NOT connect any GPIO pin to 5V.

Button            BCM GPIO     Physical header pin     Other button leg
Welcome           GPIO 5       Pin 29                  GND
View 1            GPIO 6       Pin 31                  GND
View 2            GPIO 13      Pin 33                  GND
View 3            GPIO 16      Pin 36                  GND
View 4            GPIO 19      Pin 35                  GND
View 5            GPIO 20      Pin 38                  GND
View 6            GPIO 21      Pin 40                  GND
Restart           GPIO 26      Pin 37                  GND

Ground choices: physical pin 6, 9, 14, 20, 25, 30, 34, or 39 are GND. You can use a shared ground rail on the breadboard.

Wiring notes:
- Power off the Raspberry Pi before wiring.
- Each button must be a normally-open momentary push button.
- Connect one leg of each button to its GPIO and the other leg to GND.
- Do not supply 3.3V or 5V to a GPIO input for this setup.
- If your button has four legs, identify the two opposite sides of the switch; legs on the same side may already be connected internally.
